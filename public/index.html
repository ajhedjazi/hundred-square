Important information

Fundraiser rules

  • Each square costs £5.
  • Prize: £200 to the winning paid square.
  • You can choose one or more available squares.
  • After reserving, please donate the correct amount using the official fundraiser button.
  • Your square(s) are reserved first, but only entered into the draw once payment is confirmed.
  • Reserved squares are held for 24 hours pending payment.
  • Unpaid reserved squares may be released and made available again.
  • Winner will be chosen at random from paid squares only.
  • Minimum age to enter: 16+.
  • Please only enter if you are based in the UK.
  • Please do not claim Gift Aid if your donation is for a prize square/raffle entry.
  • The winner will be contacted using the details provided.
  • The winner's name will only be shared publicly with permission.
  • If all squares do not sell, the organiser may still proceed with the draw using paid entries only or extend the fundraiser.
  • Draw date/time to be confirmed by the organiser.
